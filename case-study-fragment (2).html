04 / CONTRACT + AUTHORITY CHECKFAIL-CLOSED RULES

Ask what the evidence is authorized to prove.

The important gate is semantic, not cosmetic. HYDRA separates “evidence exists” from “the evidence authorizes this conclusion.” A classifier can enrich an existing Constraint, but it cannot create canonical existence, identity, or a stronger scope than the evidence supports.

IDENTITY RULEClassification may refine an existing Constraint. It may not create one or promote a candidate occurrence into canonical identity.
SCOPE RULEMultiple sources discussing the same phenomenon do not, by themselves, authorize a SYSTEMIC classification.
SCARCITY RULEPricing pressure alone does not authorize a PHYSICAL SHORTAGE conclusion.
AVAILABILITY RULEPhysical availability and qualified availability remain distinct concepts.

06 / CONSTRAINT INTERPRETATIONBOUNDED SEMANTICS

The system chooses the narrowest conclusion the evidence can defend.

Evidence supports a constrained, venue/segment-specific limitation with partial qualified availability and observed pricing pressure.

NOT AUTHORIZED SYSTEMIC SHORTAGE

Broader scarcity is not established. Price pressure does not prove physical scarcity, and narrow source coverage does not prove systemic scope.

WHY THIS IS A DATA-ENGINEERING STORY

The value is not the label “bottleneck.” The value is the controlled path that makes the label inspectable: source accounting, normalized records, stable identity, contract gates, provenance, history, and an explicit refusal to overstate evidence.

WHAT HYDRA DOES WHEN AUTHORITY IS MISSING

Integrated CI-008 evidence demonstrates the same policy mechanically: when required handoff fields cannot be supplied from authoritative case data, semantic values stay uninvented and the lane blocks instead of manufacturing a green result.

RECRUITER TAKEAWAY HYDRA turns market evidence into traceable intelligence by controlling identity, authority, provenance, and scope before a conclusion is allowed to travel downstream.
