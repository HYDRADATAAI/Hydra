TECHNICAL PROOF / 30-90 SECOND PATH

Correctness is part of the pipeline.

HYDRA turns fragmented market inputs into controlled, traceable structures, then forces each handoff through explicit contracts and evidence gates. A failed seam is not painted green: prove the defect, repair only what is justified, rerun, or block when authority is missing.
