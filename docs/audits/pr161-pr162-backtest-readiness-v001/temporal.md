## Minimum missing evidence

The following is a minimum intake specification derived from the current boundaries, not an implemented positive-proof schema or an authorization to change admission:

- **Claim and cutoff:** packet_id; case_id or governed claim_id; slice_id or corpus case_id; exact historical cutoff with timezone; claimed availability instant with timezone and precision; evidence kind and scope: public version availability versus HYDRA acquisition. A historical cutoff claim must be explicit; publication, acquisition, effective time and known_at remain distinct.
- **Exact version identity:** source_id; canonical locator plus immutable version locator if available; source_version_id; artifact SHA-256 and byte length; preserved raw bytes or independently accessible immutable exact-version reference; representation/content-type and any normalization/transformation link. Proof must bind the exact version; a mutable URL or normalized-summary digest alone is insufficient.
- **Contemporaneous witness:** original timestamp token or archive/witness record bytes; proof format/version; witness observation or issuance time with timezone; binding from witness to exact artifact digest/version; evidence supporting availability no later than cutoff. New capture/present hash cannot retroactively prove the earlier observation.
- **Authenticated trust and reproducible verification:** authority/verifier identity and explicit designation record; accepted verification algorithm and policy; trusted certificate/key/fingerprint or equivalent authenticated archive trust material; applicable chain and revocation/supersession evidence; independently reproducible verification procedure/tool/version and result. Self-declared VERIFIED or a signature without identified trusted authority cannot create historical time proof; current repository has no positive timestamp-proof extension.
